**Stropaviljoen -  Heesch, NL**

![Local Shit Construction](/images/stropaviljoen.jpg)
![Local Shit Construction](/images/Fotostrohuis.jpg)

In combination with the first Local Shit workshop we had the chance to plaster the interior walls of the Stropaviljoen of architect Lydia Fraaie in Heesch. The interior walls that were covered had a measurement of about 20 sqm. For the plaster we used locally dug clay,  cow manure from Biologische boerderij Fam. Van der Wijst and prewashed sand. 
The plaster was applied on primed osb sheets in two layers. In the first rough layer the plaster grid was integrated (during the workshop), while the second layer was applied thinly after the first layer dried completely.
