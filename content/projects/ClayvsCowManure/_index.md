---
title: "Clay vs Cow Manure"
type: docs
---


These three panels demonstrate the waterproofing effect of cow manure on clay. During the exhibition at DDW 2024, visitors were able to spray water on the three panels, of which two were made with **Local Shit Plaster** next to one from conventional clay plaster. While the clay plaster immediatly dissolved, the Local Shit Panels withstood the water and dried up without any visible remarks.
